# WhatsApp Bot Platform - User Guide

Welcome to WhatsBot! This guide will help you get started with creating and managing your WhatsApp bots.

## Getting Started

### 1. Create an Account
- Visit the landing page and click "Get Started"
- Enter your email and create a strong password
- Confirm your email address through the link sent to your inbox
- Log in to your dashboard

### 2. Create Your First Bot

Once logged in:

1. Click the **"Create Bot"** button in the top right
2. Enter your bot's name (e.g., "Customer Support Bot")
3. Add a description of what your bot will do
4. Click **"Create Bot"**

You'll be redirected to your bot's dashboard.

### 3. Connect to WhatsApp

In your bot's overview tab:

1. Click **"Connect WhatsApp"** button
2. A modal will appear with a QR code
3. Open WhatsApp on your device (the number you want the bot to use)
4. Go to **Settings → Linked Devices**
5. Click **"Link a Device"**
6. Scan the QR code displayed in the platform
7. Wait for the connection to establish

When successful, you'll see a **"Connection Successful!"** message.

### 4. Configure Your Bot

Once connected, you can configure:

#### In the **Settings** tab:
- **Bot Name** - Display name for your bot
- **Description** - What your bot does
- **Auto-Reply Enabled** - Toggle automatic responses
- **Greeting Message** - Message sent when someone first contacts the bot

Click **"Save Settings"** to apply changes.

#### In the **Rules** tab:
- Create automated responses based on keywords
- Set triggers and corresponding responses
- Enable or disable rules without deleting them

#### In the **Messages** tab:
- View all incoming and outgoing messages
- See conversation history
- Track bot activity

#### In the **Contacts** tab:
- View all contacts that have messaged your bot
- See interaction history per contact

## Features

### Auto-Reply System
Your bot can automatically respond to incoming messages based on configured rules:

1. Go to the **Rules** tab
2. Click **"Create Rule"**
3. Set a **Trigger** (keyword the message must contain)
4. Set a **Response** (what the bot will send)
5. Click **"Create"**

The rule becomes active immediately.

### Real-time Monitoring
- **Messages tab**: Monitor all conversations in real-time
- **Bot Status**: See if your bot is online/offline
- **Message Count**: Track daily message volume

### Settings Management
Customize your bot's behavior without code:
- Change greeting messages
- Toggle auto-reply on/off
- Update bot description
- Modify settings instantly

## Best Practices

1. **Clear Bot Purpose** - Use descriptive names and descriptions
2. **Relevant Rules** - Create rules for common customer questions
3. **Friendly Messages** - Keep automated responses friendly and helpful
4. **Regular Monitoring** - Check messages regularly for any issues
5. **Test Before Going Live** - Test your bot with a friend first

## Troubleshooting

### Bot Won't Connect
- Make sure WhatsApp is properly installed on your device
- Check your internet connection
- Try scanning the QR code again
- Ensure you're using the correct WhatsApp account

### Bot Not Responding
- Check if auto-reply is enabled in Settings
- Verify rules are created and enabled
- Check the Messages tab to see if messages are arriving
- Ensure greeting message is configured

### Connection Lost
- Reconnect via the WhatsApp QR code
- Check if WhatsApp is still running on your device
- Review your internet connection stability

## Account Management

### Update Profile
Go to **Settings** in the dashboard to update your account information.

### Delete a Bot
1. Open the bot's Settings tab
2. Scroll to "Danger Zone"
3. Click **"Delete Bot"**
4. Confirm the deletion

**Note**: This action is irreversible.

### Sign Out
Click your profile menu and select "Sign Out" to disconnect from your account.
